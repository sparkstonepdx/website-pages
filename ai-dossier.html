Subject Dossier // Field Readout

What does your AI know about you?

Run the prompt on your assistant. Paste back whatever it says. You get the same clean dossier no matter which model your friend used, so you can line them up and see whose AI is keeping the thickest file.

Step 01

Copy the prompt

Send it to your AI. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, any of them.

Step 02

Paste the reply

Drop in the JSON your AI returned. It builds the card automatically.

Share drops the image into any app on your phone. Copy link reopens this exact card for whoever you send it to.

Notice Nothing you paste or make here is collected or sent to a server. Your dossier stays in your browser, and a shared link carries it only to the people you choose to send it to. This is a game, meant for fun and information only. An AI guessing about you gets plenty wrong, so do not take any of it as fact.
